# Key Terms for Exploring the Data Distribution

| Term | Definition | Synonyms | Image |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boxplot** | A plot introduced by Tukey as a quick way to visualize the distribution of data. | box and whiskers plot | ![Boxplot](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1a/Boxplot_vs_PDF.svg) ( |
| **Frequency table** | A tally of counts of numeric values that fall into bins. | — | ![Frequency Table](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8c/Grouped_Data_Frequency_Table.svg) |
| **Histogram** | A plot of a frequency table with bins on the x-axis and counts on the y-axis. | — | ![Histogram](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8e/Histogram_example.svg) |
| **Density plot** | A smoothed version of a histogram based on kernel density estimation. | — | ![Density Plot](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/Kernel_density_estimation.png) |

## KEY TERMS FOR CORRELATION

| Term | Definition | Image |
|------|------------|-------|
| Correlation coefficient | A metric that measures the extent to which numeric variables are associated with one another (ranges from –1 to +1). | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/88/Correlation_examples2.svg" width="160"> |
| Correlation matrix | A table where variables are shown on both rows and columns, and the cell values are the correlations between variables. | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2a/Correlation_matrix.png" width="160"> |
| Scatterplot | A plot where the x-axis represents one variable and the y-axis represents another variable. | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7f/Scatter_diagram_for_quality_characteristic_-_Defect_concentration.svg" width="160"> |


## KEY TERMS FOR EXPLORING TWO OR MORE VARIABLES

| Term | Definition | Image |
|------|------------|-------|
| Contingency table | A tally of counts between two or more categorical variables. | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5b/Contingency_table.svg" width="160"> |
| Hexagonal binning | A plot of two numeric variables with data grouped into hexagons. | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3a/Hexbin_example.png" width="160"> |
| Contour plot | A density plot of two numeric variables shown like a topographic map. | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7b/Contour_plot_example.png" width="160"> |
| Violin plot | A boxplot-like visualization that shows data distribution and density. | <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1a/Violin_plot_example.png" width="160"> |
